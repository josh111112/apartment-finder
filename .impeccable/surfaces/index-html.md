# Surface Brief: Home Threshold & Listings Shell (`index.html` / `src/web/App.tsx`)

- **Mode:** Experience
- **Scope:** Scroll-driven entry threshold (closed bungalow door → open door → zoom into living room backdrop) leading into the foreground rental listings workspace shell (backend data wiring deferred to next pass).
- **Audience & Task:** Personal renter exploring Grand Rapids Westside (49504 / Bridge Street & West of the River) duplexes, townhomes, flats, and houses.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Step physically through the front door of a Westside Grand Rapids bungalow into its sunlit living room, where the room itself becomes the calm atmospheric backdrop behind the rental search surface — refusing the generic real-estate split hero and apartment-complex card wall.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Sampled directly from the user's three editorial ink-line illustrations: seamless `#FFFFFF` exterior ground transitioning to sunlit cream `#FAF4E3` interior surfaces, deep navy ink `#1B264F` for typography and crisp 1.5px architectural hairlines, terracotta `#B84323` for primary actions and active states, sky-arch blue `#7EDDF7` and honey oak `#C47B3E` as structural accents; `Bricolage Grotesque` display voice paired with `Source Sans 3` UI copy and `JetBrains Mono` strictly for tabular numbers/ZIP/dates.
- **STORY:** The visitor arrives on a quiet white threshold facing the closed front door, feels the physical invitation as scrolling swings the door inward and pulls them through the archway into the warm living room, and lands smoothly on the listings workspace floating cleanly in front of the room backdrop.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Centered full-bleed white canvas holding the closed-door bungalow illustration at generous scale, a concise editorial `h1` headline set in deep navy ink directly above the sky arch, a single terracotta "Step inside" action + scroll cue anchored cleanly below the porch steps, and a minimal top bar with the site wordmark and 49504 location pill.
- **FORM:** User-pinned scroll-threshold illustrated bungalow world raised by kinetic-sculpture-automata-crank-paper-menagerie (continuous scroll-linked mechanical stage timing with crisp illustrated keyframes and accessible step controls); `seed:5193c1affbb09f2c` (pinned words: "start with this image of the door closed, then as you scroll down the door opens and finally once reaching the listings the view zooms into the apartment... the zoom in will be the background and then we will have the backend data be populated infront of it").
